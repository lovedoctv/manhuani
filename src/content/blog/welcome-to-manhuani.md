Welcome to **Manhuani**, a home for readers who love manhua and manhwa.

## What you'll find here

- **Latest updates** for the series you follow, right on the homepage.
- **Series guides** with synopsis, genres, status and the full chapter list.
- **Reading lists** to help you find your next favourite story by genre or mood.

## Following a series

Open any series and tap **☆ Bookmark**. Your bookmarks are saved in your browser and listed on the [Bookmarks](/bookmarks/) page.

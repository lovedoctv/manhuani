Cultivation stories follow characters who train their body and spirit to climb towards immortality. Here are five series on the site that make a great starting point.

1. **[Sword Saint of the Ninth Heaven](/manga/sword-saint-of-the-ninth-heaven/)**: classic underdog story with a sword that remembers past lives.
2. **[Thousand-Year Alchemist Returns](/manga/thousand-year-alchemist-returns/)**: reincarnation plus pill-making rivalries.
3. **[Emperor of the Silent Sect](/manga/emperor-of-the-silent-sect/)**: sect politics with a lead who never speaks.
4. **[Spirit Beast Tamer of the Wastes](/manga/spirit-beast-tamer-of-the-wastes/)**: adventure-heavy with great creature designs.
5. **[Reincarnated as the Sect Cat](/manga/reincarnated-as-the-sect-cat/)**: a lighter, funny take on the genre.

Browse the whole [Cultivation genre](/manga-genre/cultivation/) for more.

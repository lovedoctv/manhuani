Australia Awards is one of the few fully funded scholarships built for working professionals in Africa. It pays for a full master's degree in Australia, and it's open to citizens of all six countries we cover.

The next application round opens on **1 February 2027**. The last round ran from 1 February to 30 April 2026, so expect a window of about three months. The best applicants start preparing now.

## Who can apply

These are the rules from the last round (for study starting in 2027). Check the new profile when the round opens, as details can change.

- **Citizenship:** you're a citizen of an eligible country and apply from that country. The 25 countries include Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, Ghana, Zimbabwe and Zambia.
- **Age:** at least 25 years old on 1 February of the year your study starts.
- **Work experience:** at least 5 years' work experience after your first degree, in a field related to what you want to study, and you're employed when you apply.
- **Education:** you have a bachelor's degree, and you don't already have (or aren't studying for) a master's.
- **English:** IELTS 6.5 overall with no band below 6.0, or the same level in TOEFL or PTE, valid on 1 January of the year you start. There's an exemption if English is your first language and you studied in English.
- **Previous awards:** you haven't had a long-term Australia Award before.

## What it covers

- Full tuition fees
- Return flights
- A contribution to living costs
- A one-off allowance when you arrive
- Overseas Student Health Cover
- An introductory academic programme and extra academic support
- Fieldwork costs for research and some coursework master's

Australia Awards scholars must leave Australia when they finish and spend at least two years outside it, so the scholarship suits people who want to use the degree at home.

## Priority study areas

The last round prioritised:

- agriculture and food security
- climate change
- foreign policy and international security
- gender, disability and social inclusion
- mining and energy

Your application is stronger when your course and your plan clearly fit one of these.

## Get ready now: a timeline

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| November to December 2026 | Take IELTS if you need it ([IELTS fees in Nigeria](/blog/ielts-fee-nigeria-2026/)). Choose 2 or 3 courses in Australia. |
| December 2026 to January 2027 | Get certified copies of your degree and transcripts. Ask your employer for a letter that confirms your role and dates. |
| January 2027 | Draft your answers about your work, the course and your plan after it. See [how to write a strong statement](/blog/statement-of-purpose-scholarship-guide/). |
| 1 February 2027 | The round opens. Read the new profile and apply online through OASIS. |
| Before the deadline | Submit early. Late applications aren't considered. |

If you're shortlisted, you'll be invited to an interview.

## Other options

If you don't have 5 years' experience yet, look at awards with lower experience rules in our [2027 scholarship calendar](/blog/fully-funded-scholarships-for-africans-2027/), such as [Gates Cambridge](/blog/gates-cambridge-scholarship-2027/) or [Erasmus Mundus](/blog/erasmus-mundus-scholarship-guide/).

Australia Awards is free to apply for. Never pay anyone who promises to get you selected.

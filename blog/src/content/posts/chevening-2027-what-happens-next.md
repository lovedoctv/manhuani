**Chevening** is the UK government's scholarship for a fully funded one-year master's degree at a UK university. Applications for the **2027/28** intake ran from August to October 2026 and are now closed.

If you applied, here's what happens next. If you missed it, the second half of this post shows how to be ready for the next round.

## The 2027/28 timeline

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| From October 2026 | Applications are checked against the eligibility rules |
| Mid-October 2026 to January 2027 | Reading committees assess applications |
| Mid-February 2027 | British embassies and high commissions shortlist people for interview |
| March to April 2027 | Interviews at the British embassy or high commission |
| From mid-June 2027 | Results |
| 8 July 2027, 17:00 UK time | Deadline to send at least one **unconditional** university offer |
| September or October 2027 | Studies begin in the UK |

## What to do while you wait

**1. Work on your university offers now.** This is where many people lose their award. You applied for three UK courses, and you must have an **unconditional offer** from at least one of them by 8 July 2027. An unconditional offer means you've met every condition, including the university's English requirement. Book your English test early if your universities ask for one. See [Duolingo vs IELTS](/blog/duolingo-english-test-vs-ielts/) to check which tests your universities accept.

**2. Get your documents ready.** If you're shortlisted, you must upload these at least **7 working days before your interview**:

- photo ID,
- your undergraduate degree certificate or diploma,
- your reference letters.

You need your degree certificate by the interview, so request it from your university now if you don't have it.

**3. Prepare for the interview.** Re-read your application essays, because the panel uses them. Practise explaining your leadership examples, your career plan and how the course will help you make a difference at home.

## Eligibility, for anyone applying next time

To apply, you must:

- be a **citizen** of a Chevening-eligible country,
- agree to **return to your country for at least 2 years** after your scholarship ends,
- have an undergraduate degree that gets you into a UK master's, finished at least 2 years before the application deadline,
- have at least **2 years (2,800 hours) of work experience** after your undergraduate degree,
- apply to **three different** eligible UK courses.

There's **no upper age limit**. Having a master's degree already doesn't stop you from applying.

You **can't** apply if you hold British or dual British citizenship, live in the UK when you apply, or have already studied in the UK on a UK government-funded scholarship. People who work for (or recently worked for) a UK government department or the British Council, and their close relatives, can't apply either.

## How to prepare for the next round

If the next round follows this year's pattern, applications will open around **August 2027** and close in **October 2027**. Use the time before then:

1. **Count your work hours.** Make sure you'll have 2,800 hours after your degree by the deadline.
2. **Pick your three courses** and check their entry requirements now.
3. **Draft your essays early.** Our [statement of purpose guide](/blog/statement-of-purpose-scholarship-guide/) shows how to tell your story clearly.
4. **Line up two referees** who know your work well.

Because you agree to go home after your course, Chevening isn't a route to staying in the UK on a [Graduate visa](/blog/uk-graduate-visa-apply-before-2027/). If you want to stay and work, look at other options. Our list of [fully funded scholarships for Africans](/blog/fully-funded-scholarships-for-africans-2027/) shows what's open now.

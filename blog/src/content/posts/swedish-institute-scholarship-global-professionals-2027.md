The **SI Scholarship for Global Professionals** is the Swedish government's main scholarship for master's students from outside Europe. It's run by the Swedish Institute (SI) and it's aimed at people who already have work and leadership experience and want to bring new skills back to their country.

For courses starting in autumn 2027, there are **two deadlines**, and the first one is **15 January 2027**.

## What it pays

- **Full tuition fees**, paid by SI straight to your university each semester.
- **SEK 12,000 a month** for living costs.
- A one-off **travel grant of SEK 15,000**.
- Membership of the SI Network for Global Professionals, and the Sweden Alumni Network afterwards.

Insurance isn't part of the scholarship, so check what cover your university gives international students.

## Who can apply

You must be a **citizen** of one of 34 eligible countries. Where you live now doesn't matter. The African countries on the list are:

**Egypt, Ethiopia, Kenya, Liberia, Morocco, Nigeria, Rwanda, Senegal, South Africa, Tanzania, Uganda and Zambia.**

Ghana and Zimbabwe aren't eligible this round. If that's you, look at our [2027 scholarship calendar](/blog/fully-funded-scholarships-for-africans-2027/), [Erasmus Mundus](/blog/erasmus-mundus-scholarship-guide/) or [DAAD EPOS in Germany](/blog/daad-epos-scholarship-germany/) instead.

You also need:

- **Work experience** you can prove. SI sets the minimum in its application documents, so check it before you apply.
- **Leadership experience**, from your current or past job, from running your own business, or from community and civil society work.
- To be someone who **pays tuition fees** in Sweden, which applies to most people from outside the EU.
- To be **admitted to an eligible master's programme** by the time university results come out.

## The 2027 timeline

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| 16 October 2026 to 15 January 2027 | Apply to master's programmes at universityadmissions.se |
| 9 to 25 February 2027 | Apply for the SI scholarship on SI's portal |
| 26 March 2027 | University admission results |
| 23 April 2027 | SI scholarship results |
| August 2027 | Courses start |

SI publishes the list of **eligible programmes** before the scholarship opens, so check that your programmes are on it when you choose them. On universityadmissions.se you can list up to four programmes in one application.

When you apply to the universities you get an **eight-digit application number**. You'll need it for the SI application, and a wrong number gets you disqualified.

## How to make your application stand out

- **Show real leadership.** Name the team you led, the project you ran or the group you started, and what changed because of it. Numbers help: "trained 40 health workers" says more than "passionate about health".
- **Connect the degree to your career.** Explain what you'll do with it when you're back home, and why this programme in Sweden is the right one.
- **Get your proof of work ready early.** Employer letters should give your job title, dates and hours.
- **Write your own motivation letters.** Our [statement of purpose guide](/blog/statement-of-purpose-scholarship-guide/) works for these too.
- **Prepare your English test.** Most Swedish master's programmes ask for an English score unless your degree was taught in English. See [IELTS or Duolingo](/blog/duolingo-english-test-vs-ielts/).

## Common mistakes

- Missing the **15 January** university deadline. Without it, you can't apply for the scholarship in February.
- Choosing a programme that **isn't on SI's eligible list**.
- Typing the **application number** wrong.
- Leaving the work and leadership sections vague.

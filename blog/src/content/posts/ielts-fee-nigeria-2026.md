IELTS is run in Nigeria by two organisations, the British Council and IDP. Both sell the same tests with the same results, but their prices for some tests differ.

These are the prices shown on their official pages in October 2026.

## IELTS fees in Nigeria

| Test | British Council | IDP |
|---|---|---|
| IELTS Academic or General Training | ₦299,000 | ₦299,000 (computer-delivered) |
| IELTS for UKVI | ₦301,200 | ₦315,500 |
| IELTS Life Skills | ₦285,200 | ₦298,500 |

Sources: [British Council IELTS Academic](https://www.britishcouncil.org.ng/exam/ielts/which-test/academic), [British Council IELTS for UKVI](https://www.britishcouncil.org.ng/exam/ielts/uk-visas-immigration), [IDP Nigeria test dates](https://ielts.idp.com/nigeria/test-dates).

You may still see older pages showing ₦266,000 or ₦75,000. Those figures are out of date, so budget with the prices above.

## Which IELTS test do you need?

- **IELTS Academic:** for university admission abroad, and for nurses registering in the UK or Ireland.
- **IELTS General Training:** for work and migration to Canada, Australia and New Zealand.
- **IELTS for UKVI:** for UK visas, such as the Skilled Worker or Health and Care Worker visa.
- **IELTS Life Skills:** for some UK family and settlement visas, where only speaking and listening are tested.

If you're applying for a UK visa, check that you book the UKVI version. A normal IELTS Academic result can't be used for most UK visa applications.

## What score you need

| Purpose | Score |
|---|---|
| UK Skilled Worker or Health and Care Worker visa | IELTS for UKVI 5.5 in each skill (B2) |
| Nursing in the UK or Ireland | IELTS Academic 7.0 in listening, reading and speaking, 6.5 in writing |
| University admission | Set by each university, often 6.0 to 7.0 overall |

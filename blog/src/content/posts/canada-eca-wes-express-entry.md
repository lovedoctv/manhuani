If you plan to move to Canada through **Express Entry**, your degree from Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa or anywhere else outside Canada has to be checked first. That check is called an **Educational Credential Assessment (ECA)**. It shows Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) what your qualification is worth in Canada.

## Who needs an ECA

You need one if you completed your education outside Canada and you want to:

- qualify for the **Federal Skilled Worker Program** as the main applicant, or
- get **points for your education** in your Express Entry score.

Your **spouse or partner** can get an ECA too. If they're coming with you, their education can add points to your score.

Degrees from Canadian schools don't need an ECA.

## Who can issue it

Most people use one of these five organisations:

- **World Education Services (WES)**
- International Credential Assessment Service of Canada (ICAS)
- International Qualifications Assessment Service (IQAS)
- International Credential Evaluation Service (ICES)
- Comparative Education Service (University of Toronto)

Some professions must use their professional body instead:

- **Doctors** (specialists and family physicians): Medical Council of Canada.
- **Pharmacists** who need a licence: Pharmacy Examining Board of Canada.
- **Architects** who need a licence: Canadian Architectural Certification Board.

Whichever you choose, ask for an ECA **for immigration purposes**. A general evaluation won't count for Express Entry.

## How much WES charges

- **CAD 264** for the ECA.
- **Delivery:** CAD 14 for standard post without tracking, or CAD 97 for international courier.
- **13% sales tax (HST)** where it applies.

You'll also pay your university for your transcript and for sending it, and that fee depends on your school.

## How to send your documents

WES is strict about where documents come from. For most degrees:

1. Create your WES account and pay. You get a **WES reference number**.
2. In your account, open **View Required Documents** to see exactly what your school must send.
3. Ask your university to send your **transcript in a sealed envelope** straight to WES, with your reference number.
4. Upload or send a **copy of your degree certificate** yourself, if your school is on the list that needs one.

For example, University of Ibadan graduates ask the university to send the transcript and the WES Academic Records Request Form together in a sealed envelope, and send a photocopy of their degree certificate themselves. Every school is different, so always check your own list.

Transcripts from some universities can take weeks to arrive, so start the request as early as you can.

## The 5-year rule

Your ECA must be **less than 5 years old**:

- when you create your Express Entry profile,
- for the whole time your profile is in the pool, and
- when you submit your application after an invitation.

If it expires, your application can be refused. If your ECA is getting old, get a new one before you're invited.

## What to do next

Get your language test done while you wait for the ECA. Express Entry only accepts certain English tests, and the Duolingo English Test isn't one of them. See [Duolingo vs IELTS](/blog/duolingo-english-test-vs-ielts/) and the [IELTS fee in Nigeria](/blog/ielts-fee-nigeria-2026/).

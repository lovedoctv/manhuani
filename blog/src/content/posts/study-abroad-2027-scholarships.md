If you want to start a master's, PhD or undergraduate degree abroad in September 2027, now is the time to apply for funding. Most fully funded scholarships close 8 to 12 months before the course starts, and if you miss a window, you wait a full year.

The good news: many of the biggest scholarships are open to students from Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, South Africa, Zimbabwe and Zambia, and they cover tuition, living costs and flights.

## Scholarships open now

- **[Gates Cambridge](/blog/gates-cambridge-scholarship-2027/):** PhD and most one-year master's courses at the University of Cambridge. Deadlines are 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027, depending on the course.
- **[Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters](/blog/erasmus-mundus-scholarship-guide/):** study in two or more European countries, with about €1,400 a month. Each course sets its own deadline, usually between October and January.
- **[DAAD EPOS](/blog/daad-epos-scholarship-germany/):** master's and PhD courses in Germany for people with at least two years' work experience. Each course sets its deadline.
- **[Mastercard Foundation Scholars](/blog/mastercard-foundation-scholars-program/):** undergraduate and master's study at partner universities in Africa and abroad.

## Opening soon

- **Türkiye Bursları:** all levels in Türkiye. The window usually runs from 10 January to 20 February.
- **Australia Awards Africa:** master's in Australia for professionals with at least 5 years' work experience. The next round opens on 1 February 2027.

See all twelve, with what each covers, in our [2027 scholarship calendar](/blog/fully-funded-scholarships-for-africans-2027/).

## Your plan, month by month

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| Now (October to November) | Pick 3 to 5 scholarships and courses. Ask two referees for letters. |
| November to December | Take IELTS or another English test if you need it. Write your statement of purpose. |
| December to January | Submit applications for Gates Cambridge, Erasmus Mundus and DAAD courses. |
| January to March | Apply for Türkiye Bursları and Australia Awards. Prepare for interviews. |
| April to June | Results arrive. Accept an offer and start your visa application. |

If you need an English test, see [what IELTS costs in Nigeria](/blog/ielts-fee-nigeria-2026/) and book early, because test dates fill up before big deadlines.

## What makes an application stand out

- **A clear plan.** Committees want to know what you'll study, why that course, and what you'll do with it at home.
- **Proof you lead.** Community projects, work achievements or starting something count as much as grades.
- **Strong references.** Pick people who know your work well, and give them at least a month.
- **Applying to several.** Even strong candidates are turned down by most scholarships, so apply to more than one.

## Thinking about Canada?

Canada is giving out fewer study permits than before. Read [what the Canada study permit cap means for African students](/blog/canada-study-permit-cap-2026/) before you decide.

Always confirm deadlines on each scholarship's official page before you apply, as programmes can change them.

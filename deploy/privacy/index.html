Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

AbroadPath doesn't require an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number to read the site.

Advertising

We show ads from third-party ad networks such as Adsterra and Monetag. These networks may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see them and measure how they perform. They process this data under their own privacy policies.

Measuring our own ads

When we advertise AbroadPath on Facebook, Instagram or TikTok, we may use the Meta Pixel and the TikTok Pixel. They tell those platforms that someone who saw our ad visited a page or clicked a link, so we can measure our ads. Meta and TikTok process this data under their own privacy policies, and you can manage ad preferences in your Facebook, Instagram and TikTok settings.

Visitor counter

We use Supercounters to show how many people are on the site right now. When a page loads, it sends Supercounters the page address, the page that linked you here, your browser type and your screen size. Supercounters processes this data under its own privacy policy.

Affiliate links

Some links are affiliate links. When you click one, the partner may record that the visit came from us so they can pay us a commission. We pass a short label for the page and the campaign that sent you, never your personal details.

Data stored in your browser

We keep the campaign label from the link you arrived on (for example "facebook") in your browser's session storage until you close the tab. It never leaves your device except as part of an affiliate link you choose to click.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings and use your browser's privacy controls to limit tracking. The site still works without cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.

5 Cultivation Manhua to Start With

Oct 10, 2026 · reading list, cultivation

Cultivation stories follow characters who train their body and spirit to climb towards immortality. Here are five series on the site that make a great starting point.

  1. Sword Saint of the Ninth Heaven: classic underdog story with a sword that remembers past lives.
  2. Thousand-Year Alchemist Returns: reincarnation plus pill-making rivalries.
  3. Emperor of the Silent Sect: sect politics with a lead who never speaks.
  4. Spirit Beast Tamer of the Wastes: adventure-heavy with great creature designs.
  5. Reincarnated as the Sect Cat: a lighter, funny take on the genre.

Duolingo English Test or IELTS: which should you take in 2026?

Updated 24 Oct 2026 · Facts checked 24 Oct 2026

Duolingo English Test
US$70, about 1 hour, taken at home, valid 2 years
IELTS in Nigeria
₦299,000 for Academic or General Training
Rule of thumb
Duolingo for admission only; IELTS if you'll need a visa or work permit that names it
Official page: Duolingo English Test

The Duolingo English Test is cheaper and easier to book than IELTS, so many students ask if they can skip IELTS completely. The honest answer: sometimes. It depends on whether you only need a university admission, or also a visa that names its own list of English tests.

The two tests at a glance

Where the Duolingo English Test works

  • Many universities. A large number of universities accept it for admission, including many in the USA, Canada and Ireland, and some in the UK. Always check your course page, because some universities and courses don’t.
  • US student visas (F-1). There’s no single US government list: your university sets the English requirement for your I-20.
  • Canadian study permits. IRCC doesn’t name a language test for most study permits, so the university’s requirement is what counts.
  • Irish study visas. Ireland lists it among the English tests it accepts for study visas.

Where you need IELTS (or another approved test)

  • UK visas that need a SELT. The Duolingo English Test isn’t a UK Secure English Language Test. You can’t use it for a Skilled Worker or Health and Care visa, or for study below degree level. For a degree, your university may assess your English itself and accept it, but only if that university says so.
  • Canada Express Entry and permanent residence. IRCC only accepts CELPIP-General, IELTS General Training or PTE Core for English. Note that IELTS Academic doesn’t count here.
  • Australia and New Zealand visas. Neither accepts tests taken at home.
  • Nursing registration. The UK’s NMC and Ireland’s NMBI ask for IELTS Academic or OET. See how nurses can work in the UK or Ireland.

How to choose

  • Applying only for admission at universities that accept it: take the Duolingo English Test. It’s cheaper and faster.
  • Planning to work, move with family or apply for residence later in the UK, Canada, Australia or New Zealand: take IELTS (UKVI version for UK visas, General Training for Canada PR), so you only pay once.
  • Applying for a scholarship: check its own rules. Some, such as Australia Awards, name IELTS, TOEFL or PTE.

Tips for the Duolingo English Test

  • Use a quiet room, a laptop with a working camera and microphone, and a stable internet connection. A dropped connection can cancel your result.
  • Read the rules before you start: you can’t use headphones, notes or a second screen, and you must look at the screen.
  • Take the free practice test on the official site first, so the question types don’t surprise you.

Requirements change, so confirm on your university’s page and the official immigration website before you book.

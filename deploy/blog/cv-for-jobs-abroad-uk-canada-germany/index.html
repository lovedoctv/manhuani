How to write a CV for jobs abroad: UK, Canada and Germany compared

Updated 27 Oct 2026 · Facts checked 27 Oct 2026

UK

Two pages at most, no photo, age or marital status

Europass builds a CV and cover letter for you

Most CVs written for jobs in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Zimbabwe or Zambia follow local habits: a photo, date of birth, marital status, state of origin, a long career objective and four or five pages. Employers abroad expect something very different, and a CV that looks wrong to them often isn’t read to the end.

Here’s how to change your CV for the UK, Canada and Germany, using each country’s own official advice.

What to change for any country

  • Cut the personal details. Date of birth, age, marital status, religion, state of origin and number of children don’t belong on a CV for the UK or Canada. They invite bias, and employers there don’t expect to see them.
  • Replace the “career objective” with a short profile. Three or four lines on who you are, what you’re good at and what you want next.
  • Lead with achievements, not duties. “Cut customer complaints by 30% in six months” beats “Responsible for customer service”.
  • Put your most recent job first, and give the most space to the last 10 to 15 years.
  • Keep school history short. Your degree and professional qualifications matter; primary school doesn’t.
  • Say if you need a visa. A line such as “Open to relocation; will need Skilled Worker visa sponsorship” saves time for you and the employer.

The UK

The UK’s National Careers Service says a CV should have:

  • contact details: your name, phone number, email address and a LinkedIn link if you have one,
  • a short introduction or personal profile,
  • your work history,
  • your education,
  • references, which are optional. Only include people who’ve agreed to act as one.

It also says you shouldn’t include your age, date of birth, whether you’re married or your nationality. UK CVs normally don’t have a photo either. Two pages is the usual length.

If you’re applying for a sponsored job, check the employer is a licensed sponsor first. See the UK Skilled Worker visa rules.

Canada

In Canada it’s called a resume. The Government of Canada’s Job Bank advises:

  • two pages at most,
  • your name, address, email and phone number at the top of the first page,
  • achievements under each job, with 5 to 7 bullet points per section at most,
  • volunteer work, especially if it relates to the job,
  • cutting or shortening experience that’s more than 15 years old.

Job Bank says a photo “is not the norm in Canada” and can lower your chances. Leave out your age, weight, height and marital status too.

Germany

German employers expect a more formal application. Make it in Germany, the government’s site for skilled workers, says:

  • the CV (Lebenslauf) is usually laid out as a table, with your most recent job and education first,
  • employers often expect a photo, though this varies by industry,
  • your cover letter should introduce you, say why you want this job at this company, and show your strengths with examples from past jobs,
  • you should attach your certificates and diplomas and any references from past employers,
  • certificates should be translated into German or English,
  • applications usually go by email as one PDF, or through the company’s careers site.

Germany also checks that your qualification is recognised for many jobs. Read how Germany’s skilled worker routes work and our EU Blue Card guide.

A free tool: Europass

Europass is the European Union’s free tool for building a CV and cover letter. You fill in a profile once and it turns it into a clean CV you can download. Make it in Germany suggests it as a starting point, but adapt the design so your CV doesn’t look exactly like everyone else’s. Europass also has job and course searches across Europe.

How to write a strong achievement line

Use this pattern: action + what you did + result.

  • “Led a team of 6 engineers to deliver a billing system two months early.”
  • “Trained 120 new staff across 4 branches, cutting onboarding time from 3 weeks to 10 days.”
  • “Grew the company’s Instagram audience from 2,000 to 25,000 in a year.”

If you can’t give a number, give the scale: how many people, branches, customers or projects.

Be honest

Never invent jobs, degrees or references. Employers abroad check them, and so do visa officers. A fake certificate can end an application and get you banned from a country. Read how to spot fake job and visa offers too, because scammers often ask for your CV first.

Ireland's Critical Skills Employment Permit: salary rules, jobs and family in 2026

Updated 24 Oct 2026 · Facts checked 24 Oct 2026

Minimum salary
€40,904 for jobs on the Critical Skills list (with a relevant degree), €68,911 for most other jobs
Recent graduates
€36,848 if you qualified in the last 12 months
Fee
€1,000, with 90% back if refused
Family
Can join you straight away, and your spouse can work
Official page: Department of Enterprise

Ireland is one of the best English-speaking countries for skilled workers right now. Its Critical Skills Employment Permit is aimed at jobs Ireland can’t fill locally, such as IT, engineering, nursing and medicine. It also lets your family come with you from the start.

Salary rules from 1 March 2026

You need a job offer that pays at least:

  • €40,904 a year for a job on Ireland’s Critical Skills Occupations List, where the job needs a relevant degree or higher, or
  • €36,848 a year for the same jobs if you got your qualification in the last 12 months, or
  • €68,911 a year for almost any other job, unless it’s on Ireland’s list of ineligible jobs.

Nurses and midwives need a qualification accepted by the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Ireland (NMBI). See how nurses can work in the UK or Ireland.

If your job isn’t on the list and pays less than €68,911, look at Ireland’s General Employment Permit, which starts at €36,605. We compare both in moving abroad beyond Canada.

Jobs on the Critical Skills list

The list is based on Ireland’s skills shortages. It includes many:

  • ICT professionals, such as software developers, data and cloud roles,
  • professional engineers and technologists,
  • doctors, nurses and other health professionals.

Check the full, current list on the official page before you apply, as it’s reviewed regularly.

The job offer

  • The offer must be for at least 2 years.
  • You’re expected to stay with your first employer for at least 9 months, unless you’re made redundant or the job changes completely.
  • Your employer can’t make you pay back the permit fee.

Fees

The permit costs €1,000. If it’s refused, 90% is refunded. You’ll also pay for your visa (if you need one) and for your residence registration after you arrive.

Family

This is where the Critical Skills permit beats most work visas:

  • Your spouse or partner and children can apply to join you straight away.
  • Once they live in Ireland, your spouse or partner can work in any job, with a free Dependant/Partner/Spouse Employment Permit.

Path to residence

When your permit ends, usually after 2 years, you can apply to the Department of Justice for Stamp 4 permission. With Stamp 4, you can work for any employer without a permit. After 5 years (60 months) of permission, you can apply for long-term residence.

How to get started

  1. Check your job is on the Critical Skills list, or that your offer pays at least €68,911.
  2. Search for jobs with Irish employers that sponsor permits, and apply directly on their websites.
  3. Once you have an offer, you or your employer applies online through the Department’s employment permits system.
  4. After the permit is granted, apply for your visa if your nationality needs one.

Never pay a recruiter to “buy” an Irish job or permit. Real Irish employers don’t charge for jobs. Read how to spot fake job abroad offers before you send money to anyone.

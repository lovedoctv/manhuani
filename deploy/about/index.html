About us

AbroadPath helps Nigerians, South Africans, Kenyans, Ghanaians, Zimbabweans and Zambians find scholarships, study abroad options and work visa routes, explained in plain English.

How we write our guides

  • Every guide links to the official page of the scholarship, university or government department.
  • Each guide shows the date its facts were last checked. Deadlines, fees and rules change, so always confirm on the official page before you apply.
  • We say clearly when a route has closed or changed.

What we don't do

We don't process visas, sell admissions or recruit for jobs, and we never ask readers for money to apply. Scholarship and visa applications go through the official websites linked in each guide.

How the site is funded

AbroadPath is free to read. It's funded by advertising and by some affiliate links, which may pay us a commission if you sign up, at no extra cost to you. See our disclaimer for details.
